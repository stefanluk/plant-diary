![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]

Keep track of your houseplants inside Home Assistant.

## About

Plant Diary is a diary for your houseplants: when you bought them, where they
stand, when they were last watered and when they need water again. Species
knowledge (light, soil, toxicity, care tips) is stored per species and applies
to all plants of that species.

- Plant overview with photo, light status and watering badge
- Diary with water, feeding, repotting, pruning and notes
- Species search with online suggestions
- MQTT sensors for Home Assistant (next watering per plant)
- One daily push summary ("3 plants need water")
- Backup export/import
- Languages: English and Dutch

## Installation

1. Add `https://github.com/stefanluk/plant-diary` as an add-on repository.
2. Install **Plant Diary** and start it.
3. Open it from the sidebar or with **Open web UI**.

See the **Documentation** tab for configuration.
